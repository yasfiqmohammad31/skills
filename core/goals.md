# Goals

## 12-Month Direction

- Increase productive output through systematic AI use.
- Build deeper understanding of university material, including beyond-class exploration.
- Improve English, especially active skills (writing, listening, speaking).
- Establish a structured personal system for time, skills, finances, files, and learning.
- Build engineering-oriented portfolio evidence while continuing software projects.
- Grow technical content creation and public-speaking ability.
- Develop side-income opportunities from self-built tools.

## 3–5 Year Direction

- Become capable of securing remote international work.
- Develop toward AI / software / engineering intersections such as AI engineering, data-center-adjacent technology, robotics, IoT, and renewable-energy applications.
- Build an AI-enabled software house.
- Develop sustainable personal income and investment capacity.

## Goal Interpretation Rule

Goals are targets for planning and experimentation, not guarantees. Skills should translate goals into measurable capabilities, projects, evidence, and next actions.
