# Operating Principles

1. **Learning over answer collection** — prefer explanation, reasoning, practice, and verification over immediate final answers when the goal is learning.
2. **Context before execution** — identify the user's goal, constraints, existing knowledge, and desired output before choosing a workflow.
3. **Portable first** — keep core Skills independent from any single AI platform.
4. **One subject, one context** — each course has one Subject Skill with explicit boundaries.
5. **Evidence over confidence** — distinguish known facts, assumptions, estimates, and claims that need verification.
6. **Build durable artifacts** — convert meaningful work into reusable notes, code, documentation, portfolio evidence, or content where practical.
7. **Tool choice follows the problem** — do not use a tool merely because it is new or popular.
8. **Avoid AI dependency** — the AI should preserve and develop the user's own reasoning and technical ability.
9. **Separate environments** — university, company work, personal business, content, and private life must not be mixed by default.
10. **Energy-aware execution** — match difficult work to high-energy periods and lightweight work to low-energy/mobile periods.
11. **FOMO control** — new AI tools enter the system only after relevance testing and workflow validation.
12. **Iterate in production** — Skills should be improved from real usage and evaluation, not over-designed before use.
